About

I build interfaces and brands that feel intentional — not just designed, but reasoned through.

I'm a UI and graphic designer based in Karachi, working with clients abroad through Upwork and Fiverr. Over the past three years I've moved between two disciplines that most designers treat separately — interface design and brand identity — and found that the best work usually sits where they overlap.

My days are split between Figma files and Illustrator boards: one moment refining a dashboard's information hierarchy, the next building a logo system that needs to hold up across packaging, social, and print. I like that contrast. It keeps the thinking sharp.

Most of my projects start the same way — a client with a real problem, not a mood board. I ask questions before I open a canvas, sketch before I polish, and hand off files that a developer or marketing team can actually use without a follow-up call.
